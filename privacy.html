Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Sager House processes personal data submitted through booking and contact enquiries.

1. Data We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, preferred travel dates, guest count, and any information you include in your booking request.

2. Purpose of Processing

We use this information to respond to enquiries, manage availability, prepare booking offers, and communicate about your stay.

3. Legal Basis

Processing is based on pre-contractual steps taken at your request and our legitimate interest in handling accommodation enquiries.

4. Retention

We keep enquiry data only as long as necessary to respond, manage reservations, and meet legal obligations.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information may be shared only with service providers required to operate the website or complete a booking.

6. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or object to processing where applicable under local law.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact sager.apart.house@gmail.com.
