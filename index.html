Your Holiday Apartment in Murau, Styria

Welcome to Sager House, where alpine charm seamlessly blends with modern comfort. Nestled in the historic village of Murau, our premium apartments offer an idyllic retreat for those seeking a true stay in the Austrian Alps. Every detail of our accommodation in Murau, Styria, has been thoughtfully designed with natural stone, warm woods, and soft neutrals to create a calming, luxurious atmosphere.

During the winter months, Sager House serves as the perfect Murau ski holiday apartment. Located just minutes from the renowned Kreischberg ski area, guests enjoy effortless access to pristine slopes, modern lifts, and snow-covered winter landscapes. After a long day on the mountain, return to your cozy sanctuary to relax and unwind.

When the snow melts, the region transforms into a paradise for outdoor enthusiasts. Enjoy summer hiking in Murau right from your doorstep, with endless trails winding through lush alpine meadows and dense forests. Whether you are seeking an active holiday or a peaceful escape in the heart of nature, our apartments in Murau provide the ultimate foundation for an authentic Austrian experience.

Location – Stay in the Heart of Murau

Sager House is perfectly positioned to offer both the tranquility of nature and the convenience of village life. A short, scenic walk brings you into the historic Murau old town, lined with local cafes, traditional restaurants, and cultural landmarks.

For winter sports enthusiasts, the acclaimed Kreischberg ski resort is just a 5-minute drive away. The local train station is also easily accessible, providing effortless connections for your arrival. Step outside your door and find yourself immediately connected to extensive networks of nature paths, making it the perfect basecamp for all seasons.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is available from 15:00 (3 PM) onwards. We kindly ask that you check out by 10:00 AM on your day of departure.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made up to 14 days before arrival are free of charge. Later cancellations or no-shows may incur a fee of up to 100% of the booking amount.

Is parking available on site?

Yes, we provide free private parking for all our guests right at the property.

Are pets allowed?

While we love animals, to maintain our hypoallergenic standards for all guests, we do not allow pets in the apartments.

Do you have a ski storage room?

Yes, we offer a dedicated, heated ski storage room equipped with boot dryers for your convenience.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard), bank transfers prior to arrival, and cash payments.

Is the local tourist tax included?

The local tourist tax is not included in the nightly rate and will be charged separately upon arrival.

Is there a minimum stay requirement?

We generally require a minimum stay of 3 nights, though this may vary during peak holiday seasons.
